MY SERVICES

Your Website
in 5 steps

Our process ensures that we create a website tailored to your business needs.

01

Discovery Call

3 MIN READ

We kick off with a call to learn about your business, goals, and what you're looking for in a website.

  • Get to know each other better
  • Determine if we're a good fit
  • Understand the goals you have for your website
02

Concept & Direction

I gather inspiration and research your competitors to build a visual direction that fits your brand voice and audience.

  • Competitor and target audience research
  • High level design moodboard
03

Design

5 MIN READ

High-end web design tailored to your brand, with attention to typography, spacing, and motion.

  • Custom layout exploration
  • Interactive prototype of the design
04

Development

In this step we bring the design to life with clean, custom code and a content management system.

  • Custom theme website
  • Modular web design options
  • CMS integration
05

Content & SEO

4 MIN READ

Finally, I optimize your content and structure for search engines, so your new site gets found.

  • On-page SEO setup
  • Site launch and handover
